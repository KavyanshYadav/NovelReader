The Mushoku Tensei Reader

A personal, offline reading edition of Volumes 15 to 26. Every line of dialogue is attributed to the person who says it, and every volume can be read as a scrolling chapter or one scene at a time on a fullscreen timeline.

What you get

  • Every voice labelled

    Dialogue is tagged with its speaker, and paragraphs that mix narration with a quote are marked quoted rather than passed off as a spoken line.

  • Scene timeline

    The focus reader shows one scene at a time with a clickable timeline of every scene and chapter in the volume, plus ← → and H L to move between them.

  • Built for reading

    Paper themes, three typefaces, adjustable size, line height, measure and alignment — all stored locally, along with your position in each volume.

  • Works offline

    The reader is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no dependencies and no network calls, so it opens straight from a folder as well as from a host.

How it was made

Each EPUB is parsed into a stable list of blocks, the blocks are classified (narration, dialogue, diary, letter, inscription, quotation, silence), and the dialogue is then attributed by reading every chapter in context — speech tags, vocatives, turn-taking and action beats — rather than by pattern matching. Scenes are given a location and a cast, and every volume is checked for coverage before it reaches the reader.

The whole pipeline is reproducible: python3 scripts/rebuild_all.py rebuilds every volume from the source books.
