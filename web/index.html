    About this edition

    Dialogue is attributed to its speaker, and paragraphs that mix narration with a quote are marked quoted rather than passed off as a spoken line. Diary entries and letters keep their own paper treatment.

    The focus reader shows one scene at a time with a timeline of every scene and chapter, and ← → move between them. Paper themes, three typefaces, text size, line height, measure and alignment are all stored locally, along with your position in each volume.

    The reader is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no dependencies and no network calls, so it opens straight from a folder. It is rebuilt from the source books with scripts/rebuild_all.py.
