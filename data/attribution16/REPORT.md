## Conventions

- `Rudeus` and `Sylphiette` are used as canonical names; `Sylphie` and `Rudy` in dialogue are kept as spoken.
- Battle cries and vocalizations are attributed to the fighter who shouts them (`Eris`, `Ghislaine`, `Rudeus`), not to a generic "soldier" label.
- The Guardian Beast is credited as `Leo`; the summoned magician as `Arumanfi`.
- Inner monologue that the classifier typed as speech is re-typed as `narration` and keeps its speaker, so the reader can still tell whose thought it is.
- Narration that quotes a line spoken in the same moment (for example "I cleared my throat. 'Ahem…'") is flagged `mixed` and keeps the speaker.
- The pub scene in the extra chapter uses `Proprietor` for the barkeep and `Patron` for the two voices that greet Elinalise.
